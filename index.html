Privacy Policy

PassPic · Last updated: November 5, 2026 (recent photos added)

The short version. Your photos are processed on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

PassPic is published by Askteam (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at tinku99@gmail.com.

What the app does with your photo

When you take or choose a photo, the app finds your face, measures the framing, crops and resizes the photo, and shows you the result. All of this runs on your device. If you choose to replace the background, that also runs on your device using a model that is bundled inside the app; nothing is sent to a server. The photo is held in memory while you use the app. It is saved only when you tap Save or Share: on Android, Save puts it in your Pictures/PassPic album; on iPhone, or with Share, it goes to the place you choose.

Camera and photo access

The camera is used only to take your photo and is switched off when you leave the camera screen. Choosing a photo from your gallery uses the system picker, which gives the app only the photo you select. The app does not read the rest of your photo library.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information. We do not run servers that receive your photos, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. The app works without an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few small settings on your device so it works the way you left it: your language, recently used countries and your unlock status. This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Recent photos (off unless you turn it on). If you switch on “Keep my recent photos” in the app’s Privacy screen, the app keeps copies of up to 6 photos you saved, in the app’s private storage on this phone, so you can make them for another country later. They are never uploaded or backed up by the app. Turning the setting off, tapping “Delete saved photos” or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Google Play (and the Apple App Store on iPhone). They process your payment under their own privacy policies. We receive no payment details from you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to delete.

Accuracy of the checks

The app checks size and layout against published photo rules, which can change. The issuing office makes the final decision, so we cannot promise that a photo will be accepted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
