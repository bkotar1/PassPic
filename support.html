Taking the photo

How do I take a good passport photo at home?

Stand about an arm’s length from the camera, with the camera at eye level. Face a window so light falls evenly on your face. Use a plain light wall behind you, look straight at the camera with a neutral expression, and keep your eyes on the blue band.

Why does the camera say “Move closer” or “Move back”?

Your head should fill the dashed oval. The guide tells you which way to move until the oval turns green. Show the tops of your shoulders too.

Can I use a photo I already have?

Yes. Tap Choose from gallery. The photo must be recent, sharp, taken straight on, and against a plain light background.

Can I wear glasses?

It depends on the country, and many now say no. Check the rules for your country in the app, and avoid glare and tinted lenses in any case.

How do I take a baby’s photo?

Choose Baby photo. Lay the baby on a plain white sheet and shoot from straight above, in daylight. The app shows the baby rules we could confirm for your country (for example, the US allows a baby’s eyes to be partly closed).

Checks and acceptance

Will my photo be accepted?

We check size, head height, eye line, framing, lighting and background against each country’s published rules. The office that issues your document makes the final decision, so no app can promise acceptance.

What does “Verified” or “Partly checked” mean?

Verified means we read the size and head rules on the government’s own page. Partly checked means part of the rule (often the head size) is not stated there, so we used the closest official rule. PassPic is not affiliated with any government.

Why did my background fail?

Most countries want a plain, light background. Curtains, windows, furniture or a coloured wall fail. Retake against a plain light wall. Where a country allows it, you can also replace the background, but some countries forbid any software change.

What does “Fix automatically” change?

Only the crop and size. Nothing is added, removed or retouched. Your face is never changed.

Printing and saving

How do I print it?

Open Print sheet, choose 4×6 in, A4 or Letter, and save. Print at 100% or “Actual size” with no scaling, on photo paper, at any photo shop or at home. The file records its print size so it comes out right.

Where is my saved photo?

On Android it goes into your Photos app, in the PassPic album. On iPhone, choose where to save it in the share menu.

What is the digital upload option?

Some countries accept an online photo with their own pixel size and file-size limit. Choose Digital upload and the app makes a file that meets those limits.
